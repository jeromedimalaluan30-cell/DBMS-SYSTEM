Philippine History

Pre-Colonial Period

Before the arrival of Spanish colonizers in 1521, the Philippines was already home to various indigenous communities with rich cultures and trading relationships with neighboring Asian countries.

  • Barangay System: Small independent communities led by a datu (chieftain)
  • Trade: Active trade with China, Japan, and other Southeast Asian nations
  • Writing System: Baybayin script used by Tagalog and other ethnic groups
  • Religion: Animism and ancestor worship were common practices

Spanish Colonial Period (1521-1898)

Ferdinand Magellan arrived in 1521, marking the beginning of over 300 years of Spanish rule.

  • 1565: Miguel López de Legazpi established the first Spanish settlement in Cebu
  • 1571: Manila became the capital of the Spanish East Indies
  • Galleon Trade: Manila-Acapulco trade route (1565-1815) connected Asia and the Americas
  • Christianization: Introduction of Catholicism throughout the archipelago
  • Revolts: Numerous uprisings against Spanish rule, including the Dagohoy Rebellion (1744-1829)

Philippine Revolution (1896-1898)

The struggle for independence from Spain culminated in the Philippine Revolution.

  • 1896: Andrés Bonifacio founded the Katipunan (KKK) - a secret revolutionary society
  • Cry of Pugad Lawin: Start of the revolution on August 23, 1896
  • 1897: Execution of Dr. José Rizal, national hero and reformist
  • 1898: Emilio Aguinaldo declared Philippine independence on June 12 in Kawit, Cavite
  • Malolos Republic: First Philippine Republic established in 1899

National Heroes

  • Dr. José Rizal: National hero, author of "Noli Me Tangere" and "El Filibusterismo"
  • Andrés Bonifacio: "Supremo" of the Katipunan, Father of the Philippine Revolution
  • Emilio Aguinaldo: First President of the Philippines
  • Juan Luna: Renowned painter, known for "Spoliarium"
  • Marcelo H. del Pilar: Journalist and reformist, "Plaridel"
  • Graciano López Jaena: Orator and reformist, founder of La Solidaridad

American Period (1898-1946)

  • 1898: Spanish-American War led to American occupation
  • Philippine-American War: Conflict from 1899-1902
  • 1935: Commonwealth of the Philippines established, Manuel L. Quezon as first President
  • Education: Public school system introduced, English as medium of instruction
  • 1941-1945: Japanese occupation during World War II
  • 1946: Full independence granted on July 4

Modern Philippines

  • 1986: People Power Revolution (EDSA Revolution) ousted Ferdinand Marcos
  • 1987: New Constitution ratified
  • Presidents: Notable leaders include Corazon Aquino, Fidel V. Ramos, Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo, Benigno Aquino III, and Rodrigo Duterte

Filipino Culture

Core Values

  • Bayanihan: Community spirit of helping one another
  • Utang na Loob: Debt of gratitude, sense of reciprocity
  • Pakikisama: Getting along with others, harmony
  • Hiya: Sense of shame, modesty, and propriety
  • Respect for Elders: "Po" and "Opo" as signs of respect
  • Hospitality: Welcoming guests with warmth and generosity
  • Family First: Strong family bonds and extended family support

Filipino Cuisine

Filipino food reflects the country's diverse cultural influences and regional variations.

  • Adobo: National dish - meat marinated in vinegar, soy sauce, and spices
  • Sinigang: Sour soup with tamarind base
  • Lechon: Roasted whole pig, popular in celebrations
  • Kare-Kare: Oxtail stew with peanut sauce
  • Halo-Halo: Popular shaved ice dessert with various toppings
  • Balut: Fertilized duck egg, considered a delicacy
  • Chicken Inasal: Grilled chicken marinated in calamansi and annatto
  • Pancit: Noodle dishes (Pancit Canton, Bihon, Malabon)

Traditional Arts & Music

  • Kundiman: Traditional Filipino love songs
  • Tinikling: National dance with bamboo poles
  • Pandanggo sa Ilaw: Dance with oil lamps
  • Carinosa: National dance of the Philippines
  • Kulintang: Traditional gong ensemble from Mindanao
  • Harana: Traditional serenading
  • OPM: Original Pilipino Music - modern Filipino music

Festivals

The Philippines is known as the "Festival Capital of Asia" with numerous colorful celebrations.

  • Sinulog Festival: Cebu City - honors Santo Niño (January)
  • Ati-Atihan Festival: Kalibo, Aklan - one of the oldest festivals
  • Panagbenga Festival: Baguio - Flower Festival (February)
  • Moriones Festival: Marinduque - Easter celebration
  • Kadayawan Festival: Davao - harvest festival (August)
  • Pahiyas Festival: Quezon - harvest thanksgiving (May)
  • MassKara Festival: Bacolod - "City of Smiles" (October)

Languages

  • Filipino: National language based on Tagalog
  • English: Widely spoken, official language
  • Major Languages: Tagalog, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Waray, Bicolano, Kapampangan, Pangasinan
  • Over 170 Languages: The Philippines has one of the most diverse linguistic landscapes in the world

Religion

  • Roman Catholicism: 80% of the population
  • Islam: 5-6%, primarily in Mindanao
  • Protestantism: Growing Christian denominations
  • Indigenous Beliefs: Various animistic and traditional practices
  • Religious Festivals: Christmas, Holy Week, and various patron saint feasts are major celebrations

Social Customs

  • Mano: Blessing gesture - taking an elder's hand to one's forehead
  • Fiesta: Community celebrations with abundant food
  • Boodle Fight: Communal eating from banana leaves
  • Karaoke: Popular pastime and social activity
  • Close Family Ties: Extended families often live together or nearby

Philippine Geography

Overview

  • Location: Southeast Asia, between the South China Sea and the Pacific Ocean
  • Archipelago: 7,641 islands (7,107 at high tide)
  • Total Land Area: Approximately 300,000 square kilometers
  • Coastline: 36,289 kilometers - one of the longest in the world
  • Capital: Manila (Metro Manila - National Capital Region)
  • Time Zone: Philippine Standard Time (PST) - UTC+8

Major Islands

  • Luzon: Largest island (104,688 km²) - home to Manila, Baguio, and Quezon City
  • Visayas: Central region - includes Cebu, Bohol, Negros, Panay, Leyte, Samar
  • Mindanao: Second largest (97,530 km²) - includes Davao, Cagayan de Oro, Zamboanga
  • Palawan: Longest island, known for biodiversity and pristine beaches

Mountains & Volcanoes

  • Mount Apo: Highest peak (2,954 meters) - Mindanao
  • Mount Pulag: Luzon's highest (2,922 meters) - known for sea of clouds
  • Mayon Volcano: Perfect cone shape - Albay, Bicol
  • Taal Volcano: Smallest active volcano - Batangas
  • Mount Pinatubo: Famous 1991 eruption - Zambales
  • Mount Kanlaon: Active volcano - Negros Island
  • Chocolate Hills: Unique geological formation - Bohol (1,268 hills)

Bodies of Water

  • South China Sea: West of the Philippines
  • Philippine Sea: East of the Philippines
  • Sulu Sea: Between Palawan and Mindanao
  • Celebes Sea: South of Mindanao
  • Pasig River: Flows through Metro Manila
  • Laguna de Bay: Largest lake in the Philippines
  • Underground River: Puerto Princesa - UNESCO World Heritage Site

Climate

  • Tropical Climate: Hot and humid year-round
  • Dry Season: November to April
  • Wet Season: May to October (monsoon season)
  • Typhoon Belt: Average of 20 typhoons per year
  • Temperature: Average 25-32°C (77-90°F)
  • Coolest Areas: Baguio, Tagaytay, and mountain regions

Biodiversity

  • Biodiversity Hotspot: One of 17 megadiverse countries
  • Endemic Species: High percentage of unique flora and fauna
  • Philippine Eagle: National bird, one of the largest eagles in the world
  • Tarsier: Smallest primate - found in Bohol and Mindanao
  • Whale Shark: Donsol, Sorsogon - "Butanding" capital
  • Coral Reefs: Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park - UNESCO World Heritage
  • Rainforests: Home to diverse plant and animal species

Major Cities

  • Manila: Capital, historic Intramuros
  • Quezon City: Most populous city, former capital
  • Cebu City: "Queen City of the South" - oldest city
  • Davao City: Largest city by land area
  • Baguio: "Summer Capital" - cool climate
  • Iloilo City: "City of Love" - rich history
  • Cagayan de Oro: "City of Golden Friendship"

Natural Wonders

  • Boracay: Famous white sand beach - Aklan
  • Palawan: "Best Island in the World" - El Nido, Coron
  • Banaue Rice Terraces: "Eighth Wonder of the World" - Ifugao
  • Chocolate Hills: Unique geological formation - Bohol
  • Enchanted River: Deep spring river - Hinatuan, Surigao del Sur
  • Kawasan Falls: Multi-tiered waterfalls - Cebu
  • Hundred Islands: National Park - Pangasinan

Regions

The Philippines is divided into 17 regions across three main island groups:

  • Luzon: Ilocos, Cagayan Valley, Central Luzon, CALABARZON, MIMAROPA, Bicol, Cordillera, NCR
  • Visayas: Western Visayas, Central Visayas, Eastern Visayas
  • Mindanao: Zamboanga Peninsula, Northern Mindanao, Davao, SOCCSKSARGEN, Caraga, BARMM
