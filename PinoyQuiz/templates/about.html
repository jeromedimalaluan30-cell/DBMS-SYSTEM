About Pinoy Quiz Trivia

Your fun and interactive way to explore Philippine history, culture & geography.

🎯 Our Purpose

Pinoy Quiz Trivia is an engaging platform created to help Filipinos and learners worldwide discover the beauty of the Philippines — from historical stories to cultural gems and iconic places.

🇵🇭 What You’ll Learn

  • 📖 History: Important events, heroes, and milestones.
  • 🎭 Culture: Traditions, festivals, customs, and arts.
  • 🗺️ Geography: Islands, landmarks, regions & natural wonders.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Choose a quiz category
  2. Answer multiple-choice questions
  3. See your score instantly
  4. Save & compare your scores
  5. Top the leaderboard!
